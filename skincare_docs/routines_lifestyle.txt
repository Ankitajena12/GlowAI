SKINCARE KNOWLEDGE BASE — ROUTINES, LIFESTYLE, DIET

== MORNING ROUTINE (AM) ==
Purpose: Protect skin from UV, pollution, and daily stressors.
Keep it simple — protection focused.

Universal morning routine order:
1. Cleanser — remove overnight oil and sweat
2. Toner or essence (optional) — balance and prep skin
3. Antioxidant serum — Vitamin C is best for morning
4. Eye cream (optional)
5. Moisturizer — hydrate and prepare skin for SPF
6. SPF 30–50 — FINAL and non-negotiable step

Cleanser choice:
- Oily or combination skin: Gel or foaming cleanser
- Dry or sensitive skin: Cream, milk, or oil cleanser
- Normal skin: Any gentle cleanser
- Quick option: Water rinse only in morning if skin is very dry or sensitive

Key morning tip: The most impactful steps are Vitamin C and SPF. If you do nothing else, do these two.

== EVENING ROUTINE (PM) ==
Purpose: Repair, treat, and deeply hydrate. Skin regenerates during sleep so this is your best treatment window.

Universal evening routine order:
1. First cleanse — micellar water or cleansing oil to remove SPF and makeup
2. Second cleanse — your regular cleanser
3. Exfoliant — AHA or BHA, 2–3 times per week only
4. Toner or essence
5. Treatment serum — retinol, niacinamide, or peptides
6. Eye cream (optional)
7. Moisturizer
8. Face oil (optional for dry skin) — seals everything in
9. Sleeping mask (optional 2–3 times per week)

Evening tip: You do not need to use all steps every night. A basic evening routine is just cleanse + serum + moisturize.

Exfoliant scheduling:
- Retinol nights: No AHA or BHA (for beginners)
- AHA nights: No retinol
- Alternate so actives are not doubled up

== LAYERING ORDER RULE ==
Always apply products thinnest to thickest consistency:
Water-based toners → Essences → Serums → Moisturizers → Oils → Sunscreen (morning only, always last)

== BUILDING A ROUTINE FROM SCRATCH ==
Start simple. Add one product at a time and wait 2 weeks before adding another.

Minimal starter routine (3 products):
1. Gentle cleanser
2. Fragrance-free moisturizer
3. SPF 50 (morning)

Once established, add:
- Niacinamide serum (safe, works for most concerns)
Then add:
- Vitamin C (morning) or Retinol (evening, start very slowly)
Then add exfoliant if needed (AHA or BHA).

== DOUBLE CLEANSING ==
Why double cleanse: Water-based cleansers cannot fully remove oil-based products like SPF, makeup, or sebum. Double cleansing ensures clean skin for treatments.

Step 1 (oil-based cleanser): Cleansing oil, balm, or micellar water — dissolves SPF and makeup.
Step 2 (water-based cleanser): Your regular gel or cream cleanser — cleans the skin.
When to double cleanse: Every evening when you have worn SPF or makeup. Not necessary in morning.

== EXFOLIATION GUIDE ==
Physical exfoliants: Scrubs, brushes, cloths — abrasive physical removal.
Pros: Immediate smoothness. Cons: Can cause microtears and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation — not recommended for Indian skin.

Chemical exfoliants (PREFERRED):
AHA (Alpha Hydroxy Acids) — water soluble, work on skin surface:
- Glycolic acid: Strongest, smallest molecule, best for anti-aging and texture
- Lactic acid: Gentle, hydrating, beginner-friendly
- Mandelic acid: Gentlest, safest for darker skin tones

BHA (Beta Hydroxy Acids) — oil soluble, penetrate into pores:
- Salicylic acid: Only BHA used in skincare, best for oily and acne-prone skin

How often: Beginners 1–2 times per week. Experienced users up to 3–4 times.
Always apply SPF morning after exfoliating — skin is photosensitive.

== LIFESTYLE AND SKIN HEALTH ==

Sleep and skin:
7–9 hours of quality sleep is essential. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep triggering collagen synthesis. Poor sleep causes dull skin, dark circles, and slower healing. Try to maintain a consistent sleep schedule.

Stress management:
Cortisol (stress hormone) increases oil production, inflammation, and breakouts. Exercise, meditation, journaling, walking in nature, and adequate rest all lower cortisol. Even 10 minutes of daily meditation helps.

Hydration:
Drink 2–3 liters of water per day. Dehydrated skin looks dull, feels tight, and dark circles appear worse. Herbal teas and water-rich foods (cucumber, watermelon) also contribute to hydration.

Exercise and skin:
Regular exercise increases blood circulation, delivering oxygen and nutrients to skin. Helps reduce cortisol. Sweat can clear pores but must cleanse face after workout to prevent congestion.

Pillowcase hygiene:
Change pillowcase every 1–2 weeks minimum. Cotton or silk pillowcases cause less friction than polyester. A dirty pillowcase is a major source of breakouts.

Hands and phone:
Touching face transfers bacteria and oil. Clean phone screen regularly. Avoid resting chin or cheeks on hands.

Sunglasses and hats:
Protect eye area and forehead from UV. Reduces squinting (fine lines) and prevents sun damage in areas where SPF may miss.

== DIET AND SKIN ==
What helps:
- Water: Hydrates skin from inside
- Antioxidant-rich foods: Berries, tomatoes, spinach, bell peppers, green tea
- Omega-3 fatty acids: Walnuts, flaxseeds, fatty fish (reduces inflammation in skin)
- Vitamin C foods: Amla, guava, citrus, bell peppers (supports collagen)
- Zinc: Pumpkin seeds, lentils, chickpeas (helps with acne)
- Vitamin E: Almonds, sunflower seeds (antioxidant, supports healing)

What may worsen skin (for some people):
- High glycemic foods: White bread, sugar, white rice, processed snacks (spike insulin, may trigger acne)
- Dairy: Especially skim milk — linked to acne in some research (not universal)
- Alcohol: Dehydrates, causes puffiness, inflammation
- Fried and processed foods: Promote systemic inflammation
- Excess salt: Causes water retention and puffiness

Note: Diet affects people differently. Observe your personal triggers rather than eliminating everything. Keep a simple food-skin diary for 4 weeks to spot patterns.

== LIP CARE ==
Why lips need special care: No sebaceous glands, very thin skin, lose moisture quickly.
Daily: Hydrating balm with shea butter, beeswax, or vitamin E. SPF lip balm for daytime.
Weekly: Gentle exfoliation with soft toothbrush or sugar-honey scrub.
Night treatment: Thick layer of petroleum jelly (Vaseline) or rich lip mask.
Stop: Licking lips (makes them drier), picking at peeling skin.

== EYE AREA CARE ==
Why special care: Skin around eyes is 5 times thinner than rest of face. First area to show aging.
Do not: Pull, tug, or rub the eye area. Use ring finger only — lightest pressure.
Pat products in, do not drag.
Use: Eye-specific formulas that are tested for the eye area.
Good ingredients: Caffeine (puffiness), Peptides (firmness), Hyaluronic acid (hydration), Vitamin K (circles).
