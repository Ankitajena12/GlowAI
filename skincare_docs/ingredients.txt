SKINCARE KNOWLEDGE BASE — KEY INGREDIENTS

== VITAMIN C (ASCORBIC ACID) ==
Benefits: Brightens skin tone, fades dark spots and hyperpigmentation, boosts collagen production, powerful antioxidant, protects from UV and pollution damage.
Use time: Morning only — pairs perfectly with SPF to enhance protection.
Forms (most to least effective): Ascorbic acid → Ethyl ascorbic acid → Sodium ascorbyl phosphate → Ascorbyl glucoside.
Concentrations: 10–15% for most users, 20% for experienced users.
How to store: Cool, dark place. If it turns brown/orange it is oxidized and less effective.
Compatible with: Niacinamide (fine for most people), SPF, hyaluronic acid, ferulic acid (enhances stability).
Avoid mixing with: Benzoyl peroxide (deactivates both), retinol (use at different times of day).

== NIACINAMIDE (VITAMIN B3) ==
Benefits: Reduces pore appearance, controls excess oil production, fades post-acne marks (PIH), strengthens skin barrier, reduces redness and inflammation, brightens skin.
Suitable for: ALL skin types, especially oily, acne-prone, combination, sensitive.
Concentration: 5–10% is effective. Above 10% can cause flushing in some people.
Use time: Morning and/or evening after toner.
Compatible with: Virtually everything — hyaluronic acid, retinol, AHA, BHA, Vitamin C, SPF.
Budget pick India: Minimalist 10% Niacinamide + Zinc.

== RETINOL / RETINOIDS ==
Benefits: Speeds cell turnover, boosts collagen, reduces fine lines and wrinkles, fades dark spots, treats acne.
Strength ladder from weakest to strongest: Retinyl esters → Retinol → Retinaldehyde → Tretinoin (prescription only).
Use time: Evening only. Always use SPF the next morning.
How to start: Begin with 0.025–0.05% retinol, use every 3rd night for 2 weeks, then every other night, then nightly.
Buffering method: Apply moisturizer first, then retinol on top to reduce irritation.
Side effects: Dryness, peeling, redness in first 4–6 weeks (called retinization) — normal, push through.
Do not use with: Benzoyl peroxide on the same night. Avoid using AHA or BHA on the same night as a beginner.
Do not use during pregnancy.
Results timeline: 3–6 months for visible anti-aging results. 8–12 weeks for acne improvement.

== HYALURONIC ACID ==
Benefits: Holds up to 1000x its weight in water, deeply hydrates without adding oil or feeling heavy.
Use time: Morning and evening, after toner.
Critical rule: Apply on slightly DAMP skin and immediately seal with moisturizer. Applied on dry skin in dry climates it can pull moisture from skin instead.
Works for: ALL skin types including oily skin.
Molecular weights: High weight stays on surface for immediate plumping. Low weight penetrates deeper. Products with multiple weights work best.
Compatible with: Everything — universal ingredient.

== SALICYLIC ACID (BHA) ==
Benefits: Oil-soluble so penetrates inside pores. Exfoliates dead skin cells, unclogs pores, reduces blackheads and whiteheads, anti-inflammatory for acne.
Best for: Oily skin, acne-prone skin, blackheads, large pores.
Concentration: 0.5–2% for leave-on products.
Frequency: 2–3 times per week to start, not daily.
Use time: Evening after cleansing.
Do not use on: Aspirin-sensitive individuals (same family of compounds).

== GLYCOLIC ACID AND LACTIC ACID (AHA) ==
Glycolic acid: Smallest AHA molecule, deepest penetration. Best for anti-aging, dark spots, textured skin. Can irritate darker skin tones at high concentrations.
Lactic acid: Gentler than glycolic, also hydrating. Great for beginners and dry or sensitive skin.
Mandelic acid: Gentlest AHA. Best for darker skin tones (Indian, South Asian) — lowest risk of triggering hyperpigmentation (PIH).
Use time: Evening, 2–3 times per week.
Always use SPF next morning after using AHA — skin is more photosensitive.
Do not use same night as retinol (beginners).

== CERAMIDES ==
Benefits: Restore and strengthen skin barrier, lock in moisture, reduce sensitivity and redness.
Found in: CeraVe products, Cetaphil, many Korean skincare brands.
Best for: Dry skin, sensitive skin, eczema, compromised barrier (red, stinging, easily irritated skin).
Use time: Any time, in moisturizer or serum.

== ALPHA ARBUTIN ==
Benefits: Gentle skin brightener, inhibits melanin production, effective for hyperpigmentation and uneven tone.
Safer alternative to hydroquinone for Indian and darker skin tones.
Concentration: 1–2% effective range.
Use with: Niacinamide and Vitamin C for enhanced brightening.

== TRANEXAMIC ACID ==
Benefits: Particularly effective for melasma and hormonal hyperpigmentation. Brightens overall tone.
Concentration: 3–5% topical.
Use time: Morning or evening. Very gentle — suitable for sensitive skin.

== KOJIC ACID ==
Benefits: Derived from fungi, inhibits melanin production. Effective for melasma, sun spots, post-acne marks.
Note: Can be slightly irritating at high concentrations — start with lower percentages.

== BENZOYL PEROXIDE ==
Benefits: Kills acne-causing bacteria (C. acnes). Effective for inflammatory acne (papules, pustules).
Concentration: 2.5% is as effective as 10% with less irritation.
Do NOT mix with: Retinol, Vitamin C, AHA/BHA on same step.
Use time: Evening. Use in low concentration to minimize bleaching of fabric.

== PEPTIDES ==
Benefits: Signal skin to produce more collagen and elastin. Reduce fine lines. Strengthen skin.
Best for: Mature skin, sensitive skin that cannot tolerate retinol.
Very gentle — suitable for all skin types.

== SQUALANE ==
Benefits: Lightweight, non-comedogenic facial oil. Hydrates and seals moisture without clogging pores.
Suitable for: ALL skin types including oily skin.
Source: Usually derived from sugarcane or olives (vegan sources available).

== CENTELLA ASIATICA (CICA) ==
Benefits: Calms inflammation, speeds wound healing, strengthens skin barrier, reduces redness.
Best for: Sensitive skin, acne-prone skin, post-procedure skin, rosacea.
Ingredients to look for: Centella asiatica extract, Madecassoside, Asiaticoside, Asiatic acid.

== INGREDIENTS TO AVOID MIXING ==
Vitamin C + Retinol: Use Vitamin C in morning, Retinol at night.
Retinol + AHA or BHA: On alternate evenings for beginners.
Benzoyl peroxide + Retinol: Deactivates retinol.
Benzoyl peroxide + Vitamin C: Oxidizes and reduces both.
Two different strong exfoliants on the same day.
Physical scrub + chemical exfoliant same day.

== SAFE INGREDIENT COMBINATIONS ==
Niacinamide + Retinol: Works well together, niacinamide reduces retinol irritation.
Niacinamide + Vitamin C: Generally fine for most people.
Vitamin C + SPF: Enhances sun protection synergistically.
Hyaluronic acid + anything: Universal pairing.
Ceramides + anything: Supports and restores skin barrier.
