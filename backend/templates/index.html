Skin Types

Find your skin type.
Own your glow.

💧
Dry Skin
Feels tight, flaky, or rough. Needs deep hydration and barrier support.
✨
Oily Skin
Shiny, enlarged pores, prone to breakouts. Benefits from balance and lightweight care.
⚖️

🌟
Normal Skin
Well-balanced, minimal issues. Focus on maintenance and anti-aging prevention.
