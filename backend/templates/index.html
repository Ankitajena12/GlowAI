Skincare by skin tone
Darker skin tones have unique needs — higher melanin means different concerns around dark spots, hyperpigmentation, and sun protection. Here's what matters for each.
Fair / light
Most prone to sunburn and redness. Risk of sun damage is high — SPF 50 daily is non-negotiable. Watch for rosacea and uneven redness. Vitamin C helps with brightening.

Universal rule for all Indian skin tones
Indian skin is exposed to intense UV year-round. Regardless of your tone, UVA/UVB broad-spectrum SPF is the single most impactful thing you can add to your routine. It prevents dark spots, uneven tone, and early ageing — all at once.

Skincare 101
The beginner's guide
Everything a guy needs to know before starting — no jargon, no fluff.
The golden rules
Start simple
3 products is better than 10 used inconsistently. Cleanser, moisturiser, SPF — start here.
Be consistent
Results take 4–8 weeks minimum. Don't switch products if you don't see instant results.
Patch test always
Try any new product on your jaw for 24 hours before applying all over your face.
SPF every single day
India's UV index is brutal. Rain or shine, indoors or out — SPF 30+ minimum, every day. No exceptions.
Common mistakes
❌ Using too many products at once +
More products = more irritation. Start with 3: cleanser, moisturiser, SPF. Add one new product every 2 weeks and observe how your skin reacts. If it breaks out or gets irritated, stop and go back to basics.
❌ Skipping SPF on cloudy days +
UV rays penetrate clouds. You're exposed to UVA/UVB year-round, even indoors (UVA passes through windows). Skipping SPF is the #1 reason for dark spots and premature ageing in Indian skin.
❌ Switching products every week +
Skincare results take 4–8 weeks minimum. Your skin needs time to adapt. If you switch every week, you'll never know what actually works. Give each product at least 4 weeks before deciding.
❌ Over-exfoliating +
Exfoliating more than 2–3 times a week damages your skin barrier, causing redness, sensitivity, and breakouts. Less is more. Stick to 1–2 times per week with a gentle exfoliant.
❌ Not moisturising oily skin +
Oily skin still needs moisture — just a lightweight one. Skipping moisturiser makes oily skin produce MORE oil to compensate. Use a gel or water-based moisturiser instead of heavy creams.
